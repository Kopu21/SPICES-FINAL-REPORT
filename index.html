Curator Briefing

Inside Home 2: The Connoisseur’s Cellar Guide

While Home 1 celebrates our core single-estate daily pantry and heritage origins, Home 2 is dedicated to ultra-rare, high-altitude micro-harvests, single-barrel wood aging, and sensory culinary intelligence.

1. Grand Cru Reserve Vault

Numbered micro-lots such as 100-year vine Tellicherry peppercorns aged in Kerala rosewood casks and dawn-picked Kashmiri saffron ingots (Crocin 265+).

2. Shad Rasa (6 Tastes) Matrix

The ancient Ayurvedic science of balancing Sweet, Sour, Salty, Pungent, Bitter, and Astringent flavors to stimulate digestive fire (*Agni*) and mental vitality.

3. Sommelier Pairing Engine

Algorithmic spice compounding recommendation system for royal dum biryanis, slow-cooked Awadhi curries, charcoal tandoor grills, and restorative Vedic chai.
