A Century of Terroir Mastery (Est. 1924)

From Heritage Kitchens to Your Pantry

For over a century, SuryaVeda has remained steadfast in protecting the ancient agrarian heritage of India. While modern commercial spice brands rely on high-speed industrial pulverizers that heat and vaporize precious volatile oils, our spices are sun-dried and stone-pounded at low temperatures below 35°C.

1

Direct Single-Estate Partnerships

We work directly with multigenerational farming families across Wayanad, Kashmir, Meghalaya, and Guntur without middlemen.

2

Zero Artificial Additives, Colours or Starch

Every single batch is NABL certified pure with zero added Sudan dyes, lead chromate, mineral oils, or spent spice fillers.

3

Clay Barni & Wood Cask Aging

Our pickles and reserve peppercorns are cured in glazed clay barnis and Kerala rosewood casks under natural sunlight.
